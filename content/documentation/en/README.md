# Install and configure KraftInk

These guides help you put your site online, connect services and keep it running. Use the forms and explanations in the administration panel to manage pages, members and appearance.

## Your first installation

1. [Prepare your hosting and install KraftInk](installation.md).
2. [Check your domain, HTTPS and email](configuration.md).
3. [Add the plugins and theme you need](extensions-catalogue.md).
4. [Set up cron and backups](operations.md).

Already have a website? The [import guide](migration.md) explains how to bring its data across.

## Connect your services

- **Game server**: [choose a connection](games.md), then [install KraftLink](kraftlink.md) if needed.
- **External accounts**: [configure Google, Discord or another provider](oauth.md). [Discord linked roles](discord-linked-roles.md) have a separate procedure.
- **Shop and voting**: [prepare payments and deliveries](shop-administration.md), or [connect a voting site](vote.md).

## Keep your site running

Read about [updates](updates.md), [backups and troubleshooting](operations.md), or the [frequently asked questions](faq.md).

Writing code for KraftInk? The [developer path](developpeurs.md) covers plugins, themes, game modules and APIs.
